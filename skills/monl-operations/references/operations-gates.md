# Operations acceptance criteria

- Overview metrics link or lead to their underlying records.
- Navigation names user tasks rather than entity implementation names where possible.
- Tables preserve essential columns and move secondary details into inspect views on narrow screens.
- Status, category, date, owner, and amount fields become filters or grouping aids when useful.
- Create/edit forms explain constraints and retain context after completion.
- Moderation and approval actions show the reported content, reason, current state, and consequence.
- Destructive operations require confirmation and refresh the affected view.
- Every role has a purposeful landing state and cannot see unauthorized actions.
